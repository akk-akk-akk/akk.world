• akk

Jun Liang (akk) is a Singapore-based designer practising across mediums such as graphic design, illustration, and creative coding.

His approach to art direction seeks to use design as a tool to find poetry within whatever project he lands in, whether its in food, print or artist projects. In his personal practice, he likes to find bizarre ways to experiment with personal subjects of interest — essentially using design as a tool for sense-making and mental gymnastics. This has included zine about cannibalism, a illustrative print about rats, and even a wizard of technology.
